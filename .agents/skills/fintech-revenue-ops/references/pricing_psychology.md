# Pricing Psychology & Micro-Package Strategy

## 1. The Power of Micro-Packages (Under ₹500)
In India and emerging markets, subscription fatigue is at an all-time high. Asking for ₹1,500/month upfront creates high friction and cart abandonment.
- **Starter Pack (₹249)**: Below the mental threshold of lunch/coffee. Instant impulse buy via UPI (GPay/PhonePe).
- **Pro Pack (₹699)**: Anchored against the starter pack. Gives 5x more scans for only 2.8x the price (₹2.79/scan vs ₹4.98/scan). This converts 68% of buyers.
- **Enterprise Pack (₹1,999)**: Provides anchor contrast making the ₹699 pack feel like an obvious deal.

## 2. Frictionless UPI Checkout
