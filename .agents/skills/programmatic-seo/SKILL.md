# Programmatic SEO Engine

This skill generates high-intent, targeted landing pages designed to rank for specific search queries without manual writing.

## Strategy

### Keyword Matrix
- `[Document Type]` + `to` + `[Output Format]` (e.g. `invoice to excel`, `receipt to csv`, `gst bill to json`)
- `[Platform/Vendor]` + `invoice extractor` (e.g. `aws invoice extractor`, `uber receipt to csv`, `swiggy zomato bill to excel`)
- `[Industry]` + `invoice OCR automation` (e.g. `freelancer invoice scanner`, `accounting invoice automation`)

## Automated Deliverables
1. Dynamic route `/tools/[slug]` mapping to customized value propositions.
2. Metadata injection with targeted titles, descriptions, schema.org JSON-LD structured data.
3. Sitemap.xml generator covering all programmatic permutations.
