# Growth Marketing & Distribution Engine

This skill executes automated marketing workflows to acquire users and paying customers without manual guesswork.

## Core Workflows

### 1. Launch Asset Generation
Generates complete launch packages for:
- **Product Hunt**: Title, tagline, maker comment, product description, thumbnail prompts, launch schedule.
- **Show HN (Hacker News)**: Value-focused engineering write-up addressing problem, technical stack, benchmarks, and honest learnings.
- **Reddit Strategy**: Value-first posts formatted specifically for subreddits like `r/SideProject`, `r/freelance`, `r/IndiaInvestments`, `r/webdev` without triggering self-promotion filters.
- **X (Twitter) Launch Thread**: 5-part hook-story-demo-pricing-CTA thread with media guidelines.

### 2. Directory Submission Automation
Prepares standardized submission payloads formatted for 50+ free and instant-index directories:
- Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, BetaList, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, Toolify, MicroLaunch, 1000Tools.

### 3. Programmatic Lead & Outreach Engine
- Cold email outreach templates customized for target personas (SMB owners, accountants, freelance agency founders).
- Value proposition and objection handling matrix.
