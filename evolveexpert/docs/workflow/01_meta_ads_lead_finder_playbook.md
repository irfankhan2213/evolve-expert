# Playbook 01: Sourcing High-Intent Ad-Spending Leads in 2026

> **Target Goal:** Build a pipeline of 15 qualified leads per day who are already spending money on ads and leaking revenue through slow websites or missing WhatsApp automation.

---

## 1. Why Ad-Spending Businesses Convert at 4x Higher Rates

Never pitch pre-revenue startups or hobby businesses. According to 2026 agency benchmarks, businesses spending ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000+ per month on Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ads or Google Ads already understand customer acquisition cost (CAC). 

When you show an ad-spending founder that their 4.2-second mobile load time or missing automated WhatsApp qualification is burning 30% of their ad spend, the ₹65,000–₹90,000 sprint pays for itself in less than 30 days.

## 2. The 3 High-Yield Niches for 2026

### Niche A: Indian D2C Brands (Fashion, Beauty, Wellness, Home)
* **Monthly Revenue:** ₹15 Lakh to ₹1.5 Crore.
* **Core Problem:** 70%+ of traffic is on mobile from Instagram ads. Slow Shopify themes cause 70%+ abandoned carts. Blanket WhatsApp broadcasts cost them ₹0.88/message under Meta's new rates without recovering carts.
* **Our Offer:** Tier 2 Sprint (₹90,000): Sub-0.5s Headless Next.js or optimized Shopify OS 2.0 + WhatsApp AI cart recovery inside the free 24-hour service window.

### Niche B: Aesthetics Clinics, Dental Studios & High-Ticket Healthcare
* **Monthly Revenue:** ₹20 Lakh+.
* **Core Problem:** Spending heavily on Instagram lead forms, but front-desk staff take 4–6 hours to respond. Leads go cold.
* **Our Offer:** Tier 2 Sprint (₹90,000): High-trust clinical website + 24/7 WhatsApp AI triage that qualifies patient inquiry, collects symptoms, and schedules appointments in 30 seconds.

### Niche C: Immigration, Study Abroad & Test Prep Academies (IELTS/PTE)
* **Monthly Revenue:** ₹10 Lakh to ₹50 Lakh.
* **Core Problem:** High volume of student inquiries asking identical questions about fees, intake dates, and band requirements.
* **Our Offer:** Tier 1 (₹65,000) or Tier 2 (₹90,000): Fast landing page + AI counselor trained on prospectus PDFs.

## 3. Step-by-Step Meta Ad Library Sourcing Routine

Spend 45 minutes every morning following this exact 4-step search procedure:

1. **Open Meta Ad Library:** Navigate to [facebook.com/ads/library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library).
2. **Set Filters:**
   * Country: `India` (or `United States` for $1,250 USD sprints).
   * Ad Category: `All Ads`.
   * Search Keywords: `"Shop Now"`, `"Book Appointment"`, `"Flat 20% Off"`, `"Book Consultation"`, `"WhatsApp Us"`.
3. **Filter by Platform:** Check `Instagram` and `Audience Network`.
4. **Inspect the Landing Page:** Click the ad's CTA button. Run the URL through [pagespeed.web.dev](https://pagespeed.web.dev).
   * If mobile score is **<60/100** or LCP is **>3.5s**: **Tag as Hot Lead**.
   * If there is no automated WhatsApp Click-to-Chat button or the WhatsApp number just has an un-automated generic text: **Tag as High-Value Leak**.

## 4. Daily Outreach Tracker Columns (Notion / Google Sheets)

Maintain these exact columns for every lead:
1. `Company Name` (e.g. Apex Aesthetics)
2. `Founder / CMO Name` (Found on LinkedIn)
3. `Website URL`
4. `Mobile PageSpeed Score` (e.g. 38/100, 4.8s LCP)
5. `Ad Platform` (Active Meta Ad Library link)
6. `Personalized Preview Slug` (`evolveexpert.com/preview/apex-aesthetics`)
7. `Contact Channel` (LinkedIn DM / WhatsApp Voice Note / Email)
8. `Status` (`Audit Sent` -> `Preview Viewed` -> `Call Booked` -> `Escrow Deposited`)
