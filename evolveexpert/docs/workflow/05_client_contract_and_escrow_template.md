## 3. The EvolveExpert Scope Shield (Protection of Sprint Velocity)

To guarantee delivery within exactly seven (7) calendar days:
1. **Asynchronous Feedback:** Client agrees to provide feedback on Figma designs and staging builds within twenty-four (24) hours through the private Notion Sprint Portal or Loom video responses.
2. **Anti-Bloat Exclusions:** Complex multi-tenant authentication, custom payment gateways beyond standard Razorpay/Stripe, and native mobile apps are excluded from the 7-day sprint and quoted as a Phase 2 project.
3. **Revisions:** Includes two (2) comprehensive rounds of design and copy polish during Days 2 through 4.

---

## 4. Performance Guarantee & Intellectual Property

1. **PageSpeed Guarantee:** Service Provider guarantees a mobile Google Lighthouse / Core Web Vitals score of **95/100 or higher**. If the staging build fails to meet this benchmark, Service Provider will optimize at its own expense until met.
2. **100% IP Handover:** Upon receipt of final payment, Client owns 100% of all intellectual property, including GitHub source code repositories, Figma designs, and Meta Cloud API configurations. Zero vendor lock-in.

---

## 5. Post-Launch AI & Webhook Retainer (Optional / Auto-Renewable)

* **Investment:** ₹10,000 INR / $200 USD per month.

  * Up to 5 new Meta marketing template submissions per month.
  * Broken webhook monitoring for Shopify, Tally ERP, and CRM changes.
  * 48-hour emergency response SLA for downtime or integration failures.
* **Terms:** Month-to-month, cancellable anytime with 7 days written notice prior to billing cycle renewal.
